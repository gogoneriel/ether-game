# Forest Gate Red — Magnolia North Gate Restyle

## What
Restyle the north forest gate with a red/crimson stone arch, decorative side pillars, and warm detailing. The surrounding forest greenery stays the same; only the gate area changes.

## Where
- North border of Magnolia map (forest-gate anchor: x 576, y 0, w 320, h 192).
- Visible to all players walking through the northern part of town.

## How
- Red/crimson stone arch over the forest path.
- Decorative pillars on both sides of the gate.
- Keep the stone walkway through the center walkable.
- Keep surrounding forest tiles unchanged (non-walkable trees).
- Day/night lighting: red stone glows warm at dusk.
- Art style: same HD-2D pixel-art as current Magnolia town.

- `docs/design/maps/mockup-forest-gate-red-v1.png`

## Constraints
- Do not block key walkable anchors (player spawn, forest gate transition path).
- Forest trees must stay non-walkable (collision).
- Stone path through the gate must stay walkable.
- Art style: same HD-2D pixel-art as current Magnolia town.

## Future hooks
- Gate color could change during seasonal events or guild tournaments.
- Could tie to forest expansion quests later.
