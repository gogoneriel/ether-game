# Giant Fountain v2 — Magnolia Plaza Focal Point

## What
Replace the current town fountain with a massive, multi-tiered fountain featuring a glowing emerald crystal on top. It becomes the visual centerpiece of Magnolia's town square.

## Where
- Center of Magnolia town plaza (fountain anchor: x 768, y 512, w 320, h 320).
- Visible to all players walking through the square.

## How
- Large multi-tier pixel-art fountain sprite (3+ tiers of stone basins).
- Water cascading between tiers.
- Glowing emerald crystal on top (matches Magnolia's green theme).
- Fountain base wider than current — occupies more plaza space but keeps walkable paths around it.
- Keep existing collision: players walk around the fountain, not through it.
- Day/night lighting should make the crystal glow more at night.
- Art style: same painted HD-2D pixel-art as current Magnolia town.

## Constraints
- Do not block key walkable anchors (player spawn, forest gate, arena gate, NPC aprons).
- Keep fountain non-walkable (collision stays).
- Art style: same painted HD-2D pixel-art as current Magnolia town.

## Future hooks
- Crystal could pulse during events or guild tournaments.
- Could tie to Magnolia City Fund lore (the heart of the city).
