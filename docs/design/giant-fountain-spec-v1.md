# Giant Fountain — Magnolia Plaza Focal Point

## What
Replace the current small town fountain with a massive, multi-tiered fountain featuring a glowing crystal on top. It becomes the visual centerpiece of Magnolia's town square.

## Where
- Center of Magnolia town plaza (same location as current fountain).
- Visible to all players walking through the square.

## How
- Large multi-tier pixel-art fountain sprite (3+ tiers).
- Glowing crystal/emerald on top (matches Magnolia's green theme).
- Fountain base wider than current — occupies more plaza space but keeps walkable paths around it.
- Keep existing collision: players walk around the fountain, not through it.
- Day/night lighting should make the crystal glow more at night.

## Approved mockup
- `docs/design/maps/mockup-giant-fountain-v1.png`

## Constraints
- Do not block key walkable anchors (player spawn, forest gate, arena gate, NPC aprons).
- Keep fountain non-walkable (collision stays).
- Art style: same HD-2D pixel-art as current Magnolia town.

## Future hooks
- Crystal could pulse during events or guild tournaments.
- Could tie to Magnolia City Fund lore (the heart of the city).
