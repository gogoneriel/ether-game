# Beach South — Magnolia New Area

## What
Add a sandy beach area on the south edge of Magnolia town, with golden sand, blue water, and wooden boardwalks connecting to the city center.

## Where
- South border of Magnolia map (bottom edge).
- Visible to all players walking through the southern part of town.
- Wooden boardwalks connect the beach to the existing cobblestone plaza.

## How
- Golden sand tiles along the south edge.
- Blue water at the very bottom (non-walkable).
- Wooden boardwalk / planks bridging sand to cobblestone (walkable).
- Keep existing collision: water is non-walkable, sand and boardwalk are walkable.
- Day/night lighting: water shimmers at night, sand glows warm at dusk.
- Art style: same HD-2D pixel-art as current Magnolia town.

## Approved mockup
- `docs/design/maps/mockup-beach-south-v1.png`

## Constraints
- Do not block key walkable anchors (player spawn, forest gate, arena gate, NPC aprons).
- Water tiles must be non-walkable (collision).
- Sand and boardwalk must be walkable.
- Art style: same HD-2D pixel-art as current Magnolia town.

## Future hooks
- Beach could host seasonal events (fishing, beach tournaments).
- Could connect to a future harbor / dock area for expansions.
